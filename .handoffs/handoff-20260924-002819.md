## The feedback page (`index.html`, one file, inline CSS/JS, no build)
**Flow: four steps, one on screen at a time** (`section.stage[data-stage=0..4]`):
- **0:** Intro + Step 1 "The two initial inspirations". It has live iframes of `a/` and `b/` (Computer/Phone views), "Things to notice" Like/Pass rows (ids `A1..A11`, `B1..B8`), and a favorite-design question.
- **1 and 2:** Step 2 "Other websites", split into Soft & clean (9 sites) and Dark & bold (9 sites).
  - Each set is a full-width carousel (`function Carousel`).
  - Each card has a sticky centered screenshot, which shrinks to 80% on desktop once she scrolls into the questions.
  - Below it: ideas to Like or Pass, a notes box, and colors ("I like the whole color scheme" plus five named swatches).
  - Tapping an idea box zooms the screenshot onto that element. The spots are defined in `VIEWS`, as `[shot, x%, y%, w%, h%]` per idea.
- **3:** Step 3 "Colors": 12 `PALETTES` (8 site-based, 4 new mixes), a 98-swatch grid, a custom color picker, and a "Palettes you picked" list.
- **4:** Step 4 "Review and send": a notes box, a Review modal (jump to any step, then a "Back to Review & send" pill), and a Send modal (Share / Email / Copy with instructions, then a thank-you card with confetti).

**Moving between steps:**
- Scrolling past a step's end fills a ring (`PULL` constants: `next`/`back` 900, `sibling` 420). The ring only counts a fresh scroll after the page has stopped at its end; on touch, the drag must start at the end.
- Each step change shows a full-screen title card, sized to reading time, with a "Let's go" pill.
- The top of each step shows "Scroll up for …".

**Onboarding and return visits:**
- First visit: a welcome box with four illustrated steps, including a looping zoom demo.
- Later visits: a "Welcome back" card (resume or start over), plus a 1.6s "Pepsoma + step" flash after 8s or more away from the tab.

**Also:** light/dark toggle (follows the device until clicked); phone haptics where supported (the iOS approach is unverified).

**localStorage keys:** `pepsomaFeedback.v1` (all picks), `pepsomaWhere` (step and card), `pepsomaWelcomed`, `pepsomaTheme`, plus `pepsoma.ageOk` / `pepsoma.cookies` to skip the demo's age gate.

**Assets:** `assets/board/{key}-1.jpg` and `-2.jpg` (960×600 screenshots), plus `brello-3.jpg`. Site keys are in `SITES_SOFT` and `SITES_BOLD`.
